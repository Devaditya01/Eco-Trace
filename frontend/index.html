      04

      In conclusion

      Our food, travel and shopping use energy and water, often far from where we live. Measuring that impact is the first step to cutting it.

      Small, repeated choices add up: walk or take public transport, switch off unused appliances, segregate waste, eat more plants, and buy less but better.
